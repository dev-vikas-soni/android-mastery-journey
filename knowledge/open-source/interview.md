# Interview Notes

## Overview
This page collects interview-ready guidance for open source.

## Theory
- Summarize the core concept clearly.
- Explain the trade-offs and constraints.
- Connect the concept to real Android engineering practice.

## How Android Works
- Describe the platform mechanism or lifecycle involved.
- Connect the idea to runtime behavior and system boundaries.
- Note the practical implications for engineers.

## Best Practices
- Use clear and maintainable abstractions.
- Validate assumptions with platform documentation and source.
- Keep notes grounded in real implementation details.

## Common Mistakes
- Confusing similar concepts.
- Ignoring lifecycle, threading, or platform semantics.
- Treating framework behavior as abstract rather than implementation-based.

## Interview Notes
- Prepare a concise explanation of the main idea.
- Be ready to explain trade-offs and edge cases.
- Tie the answer back to production concerns.
